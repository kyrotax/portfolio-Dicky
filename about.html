Who I am

Dicky Karillo
Redinata

I'm an Informatics undergraduate at UPN "Veteran" Jawa Timur — a university with a problem-solving culture I happen to agree with. I build things at the intersection of AI, full-stack engineering, and design. Not because it's trendy, but because I genuinely can't stop thinking about what's possible when these disciplines actually work together.

My projects range from gamified EdTech platforms with early dyslexia detection to AI-powered civic tech for waste mapping to premium car showroom UIs that don't look like they were built in five minutes. I care about the whole product — not just the part that shows up in a screenshot.

When I'm not coding, I'm probably explaining why something can be done, leading a team that initially disagreed, or debugging something at an unreasonable hour. I'm comfortable in the messy middle of ambitious projects — and honestly, that's where the interesting problems live anyway.
